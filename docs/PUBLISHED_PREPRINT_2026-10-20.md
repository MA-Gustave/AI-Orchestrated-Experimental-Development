## Scope

The preprint advances the automation-driven abstraction-shift interpretation of human-directed AI experimental development. Its case study reports development in fewer than 14 days with ChatGPT Plus and without prior specialist expertise in simdjson, RISC-V or RVV (author's account), together with native performance evidence.

The final Test 106 metrics are 1.6007× Cloud-V and 1.8946× EPCC **against official simdjson 5.0.4 fallback**, not against every simdjson backend. These are workloads and configurations studied, not universal superiority. The tiny-valid-JSON regression and historical Test 107 `HOLD_CI_AND_RELEASE_SIGNOFF` remain part of the record. A direct controlled AI-vs-no-AI causal study has not been performed.

For experimental traces, consult [final native results](FINAL_NATIVE_RESULTS_2026-10-18.md), [evidence ledger](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md), [VLA-MethodLab](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab) and [VectorPath-RVV](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VectorPath-RVV). The preprint DOI does **not** separately archive the original Test 106 native raw ZIPs or imply a frozen software release.
