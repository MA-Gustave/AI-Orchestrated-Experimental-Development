# Independent research governance, licensing and confidential direction

**Status:** public research-policy statement · 14 October 2026. This statement is informational and does not replace a software licence or amend an existing grant.

## AI-assisted work and upstream contribution rules

The upstream [simdjson AI Usage Policy](https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson/blob/master/AI_USAGE_POLICY.md) **does not categorically prohibit AI-assisted contributions**. It permits contributors to use AI tools but requires a human in the loop: the submitter must personally read and review generated material before asking for review, understand the proposed change, answer questions and remain accountable. Its policy characterizes unreviewed LLM submissions as *extractive contributions* because reviewing them imposes work on maintainers.

The approach documented in **[VLA-MethodLab](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab)** uses AI extensively to assist hypothesis formulation, implementation, experimental orchestration and reporting, subject to explicit human direction and empirical verification. Differences in governance, contribution expectations and research priorities are a reason to maintain the laboratory and its resulting **[VectorPath-RVV](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VectorPath-RVV)** implementation as **independent projects**, rather than seeking upstream incorporation. This is a choice about project independence, **not** an assertion that simdjson refuses all AI-assisted code or that its maintainers endorsed this research.

The projects' independent governance is intended to accommodate openly disclosed AI-assisted experimentation, including contributions with documented human accountability, provenance, reproducible tests and correction of failures. No assertion about the motives or conduct of any particular person is made here.

## Longer-term semantic ecosystem — nonpublic roadmap

A possible longer-term research direction concerns **integration of technologies developed here into a broader semantic software ecosystem**. This is a prospective direction, **not** an announced architecture, product, integration milestone or proven capability.

The technical roadmap, possible architectural connections, implementation sequencing and related internal research material are **not part of the public documentation**. Any nonpublic information is intended to be disclosed selectively to **core collaborators directly participating in the relevant work**, on a need-to-know basis and subject to appropriate arrangements. No public timeline or technical specification is committed here.

Public evidence and reproducibility standards still apply to claims that are actually published. The confidentiality of potential future work must not be used as a substitute for verifiable results in the present research paper.
