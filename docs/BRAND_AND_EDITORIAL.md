# Research identity and editorial direction

**Research / 001 — Abstract Mechanisms · by Gustave.**  
**Signature:** *Ideas made operational.*

This repository uses an intentionally restrained part of the Abstract Mechanisms identity so that the scholarly argument remains the focus.

## Visual principles

- Warm paper (`#F3EFE4`) and near-black ink (`#1B1C1A`) as the dominant surfaces.
- Prussian blue (`#24455B`) for structural links; mineral vermilion (`#D95B38`) for decisions; sage (`#91A18C`) for states; ochre (`#C4A25A`) for measurements.
- Clear explanatory diagrams. Every element should correspond to a real research step or technical mechanism.
- No steampunk machinery, decorative gears, occult symbols, generic neon AI imagery or imaginary laboratory team.
- The supplied [Abstract Mechanisms mark](../assets/abstract-mechanisms-mark.svg) is preserved as a brand asset. The original visual identity is not presented as a scholarly validation seal.

## Textual voice

- Short claim, concrete explanation, evidence and limitations.
- Precise about what was implemented, what was measured and what is only proposed.
- Calm and direct rather than visionary or promotional.
- Primary public identity: Gustave. Avoid fictional biographical or institutional affiliations.

## Scope

The manuscript is the research contribution; branding is a modest signature. Source and data should always be more prominent than branding when presenting numerical claims.
