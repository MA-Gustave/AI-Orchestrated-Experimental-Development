# Research independence and software rights

The research manuscript documents a method. The two independently governed technical projects are [VLA-MethodLab](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab) (experimental method and validation) and [VectorPath-RVV](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VectorPath-RVV) (implementation).

The [upstream simdjson AI usage policy](https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson/blob/master/AI_USAGE_POLICY.md) allows reviewed, human-accountable AI-assisted contributions; it does **not** impose a blanket prohibition on their use. This research adopts its own project governance instead of presenting these projects as upstream contributions.

Publication here does **not** grant new permission to incorporate qualifying, independently owned, rights-reserved original additions from the two software projects into upstream simdjson (or another project), except as already authorized under applicable licences or law. Written permission from the relevant rights holder is required when such authorization is otherwise absent. Existing MIT/Apache rights and third-party licences remain unaffected, and the software projects' current provenance registers do not yet establish any particular newly restricted file or addition.

A potential longer-term integration into a broader semantic software ecosystem is being considered. Its technical roadmap is nonpublic and intended only for directly involved core collaborators, under appropriate arrangements. No specific implementation, milestone or result is publicly claimed.
