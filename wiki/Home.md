# AI-Orchestrated Experimental Development

**A research project by Gustave · Abstract Mechanisms**  
*Ideas made operational.*

This wiki accompanies a research paper on the shift of human effort toward higher levels of abstraction when implementation and experimentation are partially automated. Its case study is [VLA-MethodLab](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab), the experimental method and validation environment; the related implementation is [VectorPath-RVV](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VectorPath-RVV).

The central question is not simply whether a parser ran faster. It is **how an operator can organize technical research through AI-assisted pattern formulation, candidate generation, native measurement, and evidence-driven revision**.
