# AI-Orchestrated Experimental Development

**Abstract Mechanisms · by Gustave**  
*Ideas made operational.*

This wiki accompanies an English-language research manuscript on automation, higher-level abstractions and human-directed experimental software development. It examines how AI assistance can help assemble, test and revise technically demanding systems while leaving correctness and research accountability with people.

**Read the paper:** [ARTICLE_EN.md](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/AI-Orchestrated-Experimental-Development/blob/main/manuscript/ARTICLE_EN.md) (available once the main repository is published).
