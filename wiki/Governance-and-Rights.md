# Independent governance, software rights and confidential direction

The experimental research program comprises two separately governed technical projects:

- **[VLA-MethodLab](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab)** — the AI-assisted research method, orchestration, experiments and data.
- **[VectorPath-RVV](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VectorPath-RVV)** — the resulting optimized RISC-V Vector JSON engine, built on the simdjson technical foundation.

## AI assistance and upstream contribution policy

The upstream [simdjson AI Usage Policy](https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson/blob/master/AI_USAGE_POLICY.md) **does not forbid all AI-assisted contributions**. It requires contributors to read, review, understand and be accountable for LLM-generated material prior to seeking maintainer review, and it describes unreviewed outputs as *extractive contributions*. This experimental program adopts a different, independent governance model emphasizing AI-orchestrated trials with human oversight and native evidence. It does not imply approval or wrongdoing by any upstream contributor.

## Integration and licensing

This wiki provides **no new permission** to copy or merge qualifying independently owned, validly rights-reserved original additions into the upstream simdjson codebase or any third-party project without the rights holder's prior written consent, **where no earlier licence or other legal right permits that use**. Previous MIT/Apache licences and original upstream authorship remain fully effective. The current conditional [VectorPath-RVV rights notice](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VectorPath-RVV/blob/main/LICENSE-ORIGINAL-ADDITIONS.md) and [VLA-MethodLab rights notice](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab/blob/main/LICENSE-ORIGINAL-ADDITIONS.md) must be read with each project's scope and provenance registers; **no specific newly restricted addition is yet confirmed in those registers**.

## Longer-term direction

Potential integration into a **broader semantic software ecosystem** is being explored at a conceptual level. The technical architecture and roadmap remain **nonpublic**, with access intended only for core collaborators directly involved under appropriate arrangements. No public implementation milestones or unverified technical promises are made here.
