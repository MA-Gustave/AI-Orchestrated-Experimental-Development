# Contributing and corrections

Contributions are welcome as **precise corrections, independently replicated results, refutations, reading suggestions, or testable proposals**.

Use [issues and pull requests in the main repository](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/AI-Orchestrated-Experimental-Development) for reviewable changes. Identify the page or sentence, provide a source or dataset, and explain the requested correction. Label whether it concerns philosophical interpretation, literature, experimental procedure, source provenance, performance interpretation, or an open hypothesis.

Please preserve reported failures and regressions instead of highlighting only improvements. Do not upload credentials, private prompts, unapproved personal communications, or unsupported allegations about contributors. Respect upstream simdjson copyright and licensing.
