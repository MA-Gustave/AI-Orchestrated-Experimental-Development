# Research thesis

**Main claim:** automation may relocate human effort from implementing individual software instructions to organizing systems that generate, test and revise implementations. The complexity is not abolished; it is redistributed across interfaces, tools, validators and human judgment.

This manuscript focuses on three connected but distinct subjects:

1. **Abstraction and composition:** formerly complex operations become usable as components when bounded interfaces and checks make them manageable.
2. **Experimental method:** a human defines goals, uses AI to prepare candidate strategies and reports, and judges the results using native tests and retained evidence.
3. **Workload-aware parsing:** specialized JSON parsing plans motivate the separate, prospective idea of out-of-band workload hints.

It does **not** claim that programming is obsolete or that AI code is inherently superior to expert code. A single case study cannot establish general productivity gains.
