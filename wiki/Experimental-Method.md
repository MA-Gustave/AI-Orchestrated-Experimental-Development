# Experimental method

This is the **publicly described** loop behind the RVV-VLA case study. It should not be confused with an autonomous optimizer.

1. Stabilize source versions, test runners, native target and evidence collection.
2. Build an AI-assisted, revisable library of optimization hypotheses.
3. Combine hypotheses into named, reproducible candidate configurations.
4. Compile and validate before collecting throughput measurements.
5. Explore many workload types and preserve surprises and regressions.
6. Generate reports, correct their claims against raw evidence, and preserve research memory.
7. Freeze policies and retest on held-out workloads and separate hardware.

**What remains human:** setting goals, approving experiments, interpreting uncertainty, reviewing attribution and accepting responsibility for published conclusions. Private prompts may remain private; measurable claims need inspectable sources and protocols.

**Implementation limit:** the publicly documented E00–E14 plans were fixed for the historical experiment. The tests were not a continuously self-directing evolutionary search.
