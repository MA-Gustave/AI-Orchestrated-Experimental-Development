# Selected reading list

Start here to understand the research lineage. These works are **related literature**, not claims of original priority by this project.

## Hierarchies and augmentation

- Aristotle, *Metaphysics* VII–IX and *De Anima* II.1; [SEP: Form vs. Matter](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/form-matter/).
- Simon (1962), [*The Architecture of Complexity*](https://www.jstor.org/stable/985254).
- Licklider (1960), [*Man-Computer Symbiosis*](https://doi.org/10.1109/THFE2.1960.4503259).
- Engelbart (1962), [*Augmenting Human Intellect*](https://dougengelbart.org/pubs/augment-3906-Framework.html).
- Bainbridge (1983), [*Ironies of Automation*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-1098(83)90046-8).

## Automated software development and evaluation

- Rice (1976), [*The Algorithm Selection Problem*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2458(08)60520-3).
- Gulwani et al. (2017), [*Program Synthesis*](https://doi.org/10.1561/2500000010).
- Ansel et al. (2014), [*OpenTuner*](https://doi.org/10.1145/2628071.2628092).
- Romera-Paredes et al. (2024), [*Mathematical Discoveries from Program Search with Large Language Models*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6).
- Novikov et al. (2025), [*AlphaEvolve* (preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13131).
