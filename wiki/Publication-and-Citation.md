## How to reference the experiments

The preprint is a **methodological position paper with a project case study**. To substantiate particular technical measurements, cite the underlying reports and their stated protocols in addition to the paper:

- [VLA-MethodLab evidence and experiments](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab)
- [VectorPath-RVV implementation and release reports](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VectorPath-RVV)
- [Final Test 106/107 evidence statement](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/AI-Orchestrated-Experimental-Development/blob/main/docs/FINAL_NATIVE_RESULTS_2026-10-18.md)
- [[Final-Native-Results-2026-10-18]] · [[Evidence-and-Limitations]]

**Test 106 qualification:** geometric-mean DOM ratios of **1.6007× on Cloud-V** and **1.8946× on EPCC** are relative to the **official simdjson 5.0.4 fallback**, across 18 public JSONs. The 95 tiny-JSON regressions and the historical Test 107 release-signoff **HOLD** remain documented. These data do not constitute an experiment comparing productivity with and without AI.
