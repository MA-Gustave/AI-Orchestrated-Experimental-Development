# Publication and citation

## Current status

This repository is a **public working manuscript and research documentation project**. It is not automatically peer reviewed because it is on GitHub. A software/archive DOI or scholarly preprint may be added after a carefully reviewed release.

**Read the working paper:** [manuscript/ARTICLE_EN.md](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/AI-Orchestrated-Experimental-Development/blob/main/manuscript/ARTICLE_EN.md).

## How to cite now

Until a formal manuscript citation and DOI have been established, cite the **repository, author identity as publicly given, title, exact revision SHA, and access date**. For benchmark data, cite the original VLA-MethodLab report and specific records rather than attributing the measurements solely to this conceptual paper.

- Main research repository: [MA-Gustave/AI-Orchestrated-Experimental-Development](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/AI-Orchestrated-Experimental-Development)
- Evidence repository: [MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab](https://github.com/MA-Gustave/VLA-MethodLab)
- Original third-party software underlying the parser: [simdjson](https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson)

The main repository holds `CITATION.cff.example`. It is intentionally a template; a final citation file must not invent a legal name, ORCID, DOI or coauthor.

## Publication path

1. Freeze the article source and its reference list.
2. Mark measured, reported and hypothetical assertions explicitly.
3. Ensure the technical artifact and its precise source version can be inspected.
4. Publish a versioned software/data archive if useful, with appropriate attribution.
5. Submit a preprint or peer-reviewed paper according to the chosen venue's rules.
