# Glossary

**Abstraction boundary** — The interface at which a human or another subsystem controls behavior without directly handling all internal implementation steps.

**AI-assisted orchestration** — Human-directed use of AI tools to propose implementations, prepare experiments, or analyze evidence; not necessarily autonomous task scheduling.

**Algorithm selection** — Choosing a strategy according to measurable characteristics of an input or operating environment.

**DOM throughput** — End-to-end throughput of the public document parsing path; not equal to the sum of Stage 1 and Stage 2 throughput ratios.

**Exploratory experiment** — Broad search for patterns and hypotheses; not a definitive held-out confirmation.

**Geometric mean of paired ratios** — Multiplicative aggregate of performance ratios matched by test condition; not the ratio of pooled absolute throughput.

**Hylomorphism** — Aristotle's account of matter and form as explanatory principles of concrete things. Here it is used as a qualified analogy to composition.

**Pattern library** — A revisable set of candidate optimization ideas and their implementation settings, each associated with a testable claim.

**RVV / VLA / VLEN** — RISC-V Vector extension; vector-length-agnostic implementation; hardware vector-register bit width.

**Stage 1 / Stage 2** — Distinct structural and subsequent parsing operations as defined in simdjson's implementation; internal measurements need not match public DOM timing.

**Workload metadata** — An optional external performance hint about document characteristics, not an alternative to JSON validity checks.
